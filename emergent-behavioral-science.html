Emergent Behavioral Science

Investigating human-AI co-adaptation, trust calibration, reflexive phase transitions, and collective decision-making under extreme uncertainty.

Human-AI Co-Adaptation and Mutual Shaping

When humans and intelligent systems operate together over time, neither remains static. Operators adjust their mental models, attention allocation, and escalation habits to match algorithmic behavior, while adaptive models and feedback loops reshape what humans notice, question, or take for granted.

This two-way co-adaptation can amplify capability—or quietly erode judgment. Over-reliance and cognitive offloading can dull frontline intuition during routine operations, leaving decision-makers under-prepared when a sudden anomaly demands independent reasoning.

Behavioral Insight: Macro-level instability often begins as subtle micro-level shifts—misplaced confidence, homogenized signals, or synchronized reactions—long before an overt system fault appears.

Decision-Making Under Extreme Uncertainty

Under deep or extreme uncertainty, probabilities themselves are contested or incomplete. Classical expected-value heuristics assume well-behaved distributions, whereas frontier environments routinely feature structural breaks, non-linear feedback, and sudden liquidity or operational shifts.

Studying how individuals and collectives make choices when ambiguity is high reveals why groups sometimes freeze, overreact, or herd into correlated blind spots—and how structured decision protocols can preserve optionality when information is sparse.

Reflexivity and Collective Phase Transitions

At the population or multi-agent level, beliefs and actions feed back into the environment itself. Reflexive loops can turn small perturbations into synchronized cascades, crowd panics, or fragile monocultures where correlated strategies exit through the same narrow bottleneck at once.

Aegis Lab examines how early synchronization, herding pressure, and trust mismatches emerge across human and algorithmic actors so systems can be designed to dampen runaway contagion while supporting healthy collective adaptation.
